The First Silence

The clock stopped at midnight, but the grief stayed awake long after the world slept.

The Last Bloom

Even in the winter soil something golden tries to rise beneath the heavy skies The earth is tired of carrying names of holding prayers that never returned yet it still opens its chest for one more seed Cold walks slowly through the roots teaching them the language of waiting silence sits inside the hours like a shadow that learned how to breathe Time forgets how to be gentle and days fall apart without warning but the heart keeps stitching itself with threads made of memory The wind remembers every goodbye ever spoken without courage it repeats them softly so loneliness does not feel ashamed Night leans closer asking the flower why it still believes the flower answers without sound because faith does not need noise Pain presses deep like winter hands but somewhere under the weight a small warmth refuses to disappear This bloom is not loud not proud not asking to be seen It grows for the broken for the ones who stayed quiet for those who loved too much and were never chosen Even when the sky collapses into gray even when hope walks with a limp the last bloom rises slowly carrying all the unsaid prayers the world was too tired to hear

Echoes of January

Whispers in the hallway, cold air against the glass, marking the time as it pass.

The Last Bloom II

Morning arrives without asking carrying tired light on its shoulders as if even the sun has learned grief The streets remember footsteps that never came back and walls keep secrets better than people Hearts grow careful with hope because hope once left them bleeding yet something inside still waits without reason without promise Silence becomes a second language spoken fluently by the lonely it fills rooms where laughter used to live Dreams sit in corners folded like old letters never thrown away never read again The past knocks softly not to return only to remind Eyes learn how to smile while carrying storms behind them and no one asks what it costs Pain ages faster than the body it learns every season by name and still shows up uninvited Some souls bloom late not because they are weak but because they were buried deeper This bloom does not shine it breathes slowly carefully as if afraid of being noticed It grows for the unheard for the forgotten for the ones who stayed gentle in a world that rewarded noise Even when the night feels endless even when tomorrow sounds heavy the last bloom leans toward light not to escape the dark but to forgive it

When Silence Learns to Cry

Some silences are heavier than screams they sit on the chest and refuse to move teaching the heart how to ache quietly The night listens without interrupting because it knows this story too well it has carried many broken thoughts Memories arrive without permission wearing the faces we tried to forget and suddenly the room feels smaller Breathing becomes a careful act as if air itself might hurt if taken too deeply Hope stands far away watching through tired eyes afraid of being invited again People say time heals but time only teaches how to hide wounds under polite smiles There are thoughts that never sleep they walk inside the mind counting every loss twice Love once promised warmth but left behind a season that never learned spring Tears choose the quiet moments when no one is watching when strength finally sits down Even courage gets tired of pretending it is not afraid Still something soft survives a fragile yes inside the no a weak light refusing to shut down This is not happiness this is endurance wrapped in a beating heart And when silence cries it does not make a sound it only makes you understand
